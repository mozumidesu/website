    AI Translate

    Translates your messages in Discord and Telegram with the AI model you choose and keeps the way you write: lower case, no full stop, kaomoji.

    • Your messages are translated before they are sent, only in the chats where you switched it on
    • Messages you receive are translated or explained from the right-click menu
    • Works with Groq, Gemini, GLM and other providers, several of them free

    What it can do

    01 / 07
    • 01

      Only in the chats you choose

      Auto-translation is switched on chat by chat. Right-click the translate button in the chat bar to switch the current chat, left-click it to open the panel.

    • 02

      Messages you receive

      Right-click any message and translate it into your language.

    • 03

      AI Explain

      For a message that a translation alone does not make clear: chat shorthand, a regional word, an inside joke. It names the language, spells the abbreviations out and says what was meant.

    • 04

      Knows the conversation

      The last few messages go with each request. After «how did you like the film?» your short «not really» comes out as «i didn't really like it», not as two words on their own.

    • 05

      Backup providers

      Up to four can stand behind the main provider. When it is overloaded, out of quota or refuses the request, the translation goes through the next one by itself.

    • 06

      Free to use

      Groq, Gemini and GLM have free tiers, so translating costs nothing.
